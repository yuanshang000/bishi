College English Assistant – Sample Knowledge Text

Core Vocabulary:
- account, deposit, balance, interest, transaction, statement, credit, debit

Grammar Notes:
- Simple Present: Subject + verb(s)
- Passive Voice: be + past participle
- Modal Verbs: can, may, must

Reading Passage:
The bank account allows students to deposit money and check the balance online.
Monthly statements summarize each transaction, and interest is calculated daily.
Credit cards can be useful, but students must manage debt responsibly.

Practice:
Choose the word that best completes the sentence.
Fill in the blank with the correct vocabulary item or verb form.
